# Transition from Prototype 3 to Prototypes 4 and 5

## What Prototype 3 Produces

- Local model benchmark outputs.
- Per-command run logs.
- Summary CSV/JSON files.
- Validity metrics.
- Model comparison evidence.
- RQ summaries where available.

## Why Prototype 4 Was Needed

Prototype 3 evaluates model output validity. Prototype 4 adds execution-grounded safety evidence and safety-latency analysis. This moves the dissertation evidence from structured output assessment toward execution-grounded safety behaviour while preserving the metric distinctions introduced in Prototype 3.

## Why Prototype 5 Was Needed

Prototype 5 consolidates Prototype 3 and Prototype 4 evidence, adds model/quantisation evidence, local-vs-cloud comparison and live Foundry Local resource profiling. It provides the final evidence orchestration and brief-closure layer rather than replacing the Prototype 3 benchmark.

## Dissertation Interpretation

Prototype 3 is the empirical model-evaluation layer consumed by the final evidence pipeline.
